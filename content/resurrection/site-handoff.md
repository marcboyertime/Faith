## 8. Concessions, Fallbacks, and Residual Uncertainties
*(The trust ledger. Each is stated in-text; the site should make this list findable from anywhere.)*

**Standing concessions (the mandated fifteen, with locations):** the Gospels are not four independent depositions (II, IV) · real differences and some substantial tensions exist (X) · the exact Easter itinerary cannot be confidently reconstructed (X §2) · the women's role is suggestive, not a knockout (VIII §4) · not all apostolic martyrdoms are historically secure (XI #2) · "group hallucination" is a bad slogan because real group mechanisms exist (VII §6) · bereavement experiences are common and usually nonpathological (VII §4) · the creed's date cannot be fixed to a month (VI §2) · James's skepticism/conversion is less secure than Paul's (VI §7) · the five hundred cannot now be cross-examined (VI §6) · burial and missing body are probable, not death-certain (V §9, VIII §10) · a composite naturalistic account is possible and is the strongest rival (XI §14) · the Resurrection hypothesis has an enormous prior cost unless theism and divine purpose are independently plausible (II §5, XII, XIII Test C) · history alone does not prove the whole Catholic faith (XV §5) · an informed skeptic may remain rational (XII, XVII §3).

**Additional concessions made by the essay:** the case rests principally on Christian sources (IV §7) · no neutral diarist / no examinable remains (IV §9, XVII §3) · Peter-alone is near-undecidable (VII §5) · the deep Paul question is underdetermined at VII (VII §8) · scene-staging (meals, touch, doors) is C-grade and the cumulative case never draws on it (VII §9, IX §5, XIII Test E) · absence alone does not distinguish resurrection from relocation (VIII §10, closing sentence ceded to the skeptic) · moved-body is the strongest mundane explanation of the absence (VIII §8) · divine hiddenness remains live and undischarged (XII §8) · the divinity inference is grade D from this tab alone, pending the Jesus-profile tab (XV §2) · the visions/appearances categorical distinction's sharpness is itself debated (XI, per Allison) · Carter/Goodacre/Cook's *Enspirited Body* cited at the scaffold's characterization only (Appendix B).

**Fallback positions (the "even if that is rejected, this remains" ladder):** if Paul's materiality stays uncertain → "embodied and transformed," never "bodiless" (I §1) · if Joseph falls → burial-in-some-form and the proclamation stand (V §7, XIII Test A) · if the missing body falls → death + proclamation + experiences + Paul + mutation remain; confidence drops; vision-models strengthen — printed (XIII Test A) · if group appearances fall → Peter + Paul + probable absence remain (Test B) · if the prior falls to zero → the composite wins; the historical findings stand unchanged (Test C) · if bare theism without incarnation-context → the weight shifts to the Jesus-profile (Test D) · if all Gospel physical detail is late → the Pauline embodied core survives (Test E).

**Residual uncertainties (XVII §4, named, never used as solvents):** phenomenology of the appearances · selectivity of the manifestations · mechanics of transformed embodiment · the unharmonized narratives · divine tolerance of ambiguity · divine-and-creaturely causation · resurrected identity metaphysics · the fate of those outside explicit faith. Plus, from earlier movements: the third-day origin (five live options, VI §4) · the disciples' expectations (the predictions/surprise tangle, III §6) · Mark's ending's function (VIII §2) · the five hundred's identification (VI §6).
